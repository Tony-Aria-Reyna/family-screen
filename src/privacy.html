Privacy

Family Screen · last updated 6 November 2026

In short: your family's calendar, lists and travel details can only be opened by the Google accounts your family adds. They're stored in Google Firebase in Google's Sydney data centre, and Google encrypts them in storage and in transit. Nothing is sold or shared, and there's no advertising or tracking.

What Family Screen keeps

  • The names and colours you give each person (they don't have to be real names).
  • Calendar entries, addresses you add, to-do jobs and shopping lists.
  • Work flights and duties you choose to load from a roster, including hotel names (which can be hidden in ⚙️ → Privacy).
  • The Google email addresses of the family's members, used to let them in.
  • For phones that turn notifications on: a notification address for that phone and the reminder choices made on it.

What it doesn't keep

  • Roster PDFs and calendar files are read on your own device and never uploaded. Only the entries you tick are saved.
  • No location tracking. “Where's Mummy?” is worked out from the rostered flights only.
  • No passwords: signing in is handled by Google.

Who can see it

  • Your family: only the Google accounts listed in ⚙️ → Family accounts. Firebase's security rules check this on Google's servers for every request.
  • Other families: no. Each family is kept separate.
  • The app's administrator: Family Screen is run by Tony Ross. As the owner of the Firebase project, he technically has access to the database, and the reminder service runs in his Google account so it can send each family's reminders. He doesn't look at families' information except to fix a problem you've asked for help with.
  • Google stores the data as Firebase's provider, under Google's own privacy and security terms.

Your choices

  • Download: ⚙️ → Privacy & our data → Download gives you a copy of everything.
  • Delete: the person who set up the family can delete the family and everything in it, permanently (⚙️ → Privacy & our data).
  • Remove a person: the family's owner can remove an account at any time; it loses access straight away.
  • Notifications: turn them off on any phone, or hide details on the lock screen.

Keeping it safe

  • Turn on 2-Step Verification for your Google account; it's what protects your family's data.
  • Only add Google accounts you trust, and remove anyone who no longer needs access.
  • Family Screen is invite-only for new families.

Questions or requests
